## WEEK 1: Feb 3-7 (Pre-Ramp)

### Mon Feb 3 — The Expense Report Truth
Expense reports shouldn’t take 45 minutes. But they do. Every week. Across thousands of companies.
Receipts in email. Screenshots in photos. That one dinner receipt you definitely had somewhere.
The companies getting this right aren’t asking employees to be better at expense reports. They’re eliminating expense reports entirely.
**CTA:** What’s a process your team still does that should’ve died 10 years ago?

### Tue Feb 4 — The Corporate Card Reality
Corporate cards used to be for executives only. Now they’re a finance ops strategy.
Company A: 200 employees, 5 corporate cards. Bottleneck city.
Company B: 200 employees, 150 corporate cards. Real-time visibility. No bottleneck.
The old model was about control through restriction. The new model is control through visibility.

### Thu Feb 6 — The AP Automation Gap
Controller managing $2M in monthly AP. Her process: manual entry, email chasing, manual payments, month-end reconciliation. 25+ hours per week. Just her.
“I know there’s a better way. I just don’t have time to find it.” That’s the trap.
**CTA:** What’s the most manual process on your team right now?

### Fri Feb 7 — The Spend Visibility Problem
“We don’t have a spending problem. We have a visibility problem.”
Software subscriptions nobody used. Duplicate tools. Recurring charges from vendors stopped using in 2019.
Spend management isn’t about saying no. It’s about knowing what you’re saying yes to — before the credit card statement hits.

## WEEK 2: Feb 10-14 (Pre-Ramp)

### Mon Feb 10 — The Reimbursement Rant
Hot take: Reimbursements are a broken system.
Employee pays out of pocket. Employee does paperwork. Company eventually pays back. Finance reconciles.
Everyone loses.
The best finance teams ask: “How do we get to zero reimbursements?” Not “how do we make reimbursements faster.”

### Tue Feb 11 — The Close Process Bottleneck
30-day close. Sounds normal until you ask: “Why?”
5 days waiting on expense reports. 4 days chasing AP invoices. 3 days reconciling cards. A week of “just double-checking.”
You want a faster close? Fix the inputs.
**CTA:** What’s the biggest bottleneck in your close process?

### Thu Feb 13 — The “We Need a Policy” Trap
Every spending issue: “We need a policy for that.”
Write a policy. Email it out. 6 months later, same issue.
Policies don’t change behavior. Systems do.
Most companies have plenty of policies. They don’t have systems that make following them automatic.

### Fri Feb 14 — The Finance Team Respect Post
Finance teams: Close books faster. Fewer errors. Chase receipts. Implement tools. Support audits. Answer “quick questions.” Lean headcount.
When something goes wrong: “Why didn’t finance catch that?”
When everything goes right: Silence.
**CTA:** Tag someone on your finance team who deserves recognition.

## WEEK 3: Feb 17-21 (Ramp Week 1)

### Mon Feb 17 — RAMP ANNOUNCEMENT
Today’s my first day at Ramp.
After years of selling ERP to finance teams, I wanted to be closer to the problems I kept hearing about.
Coming in as a student. Listening more than talking. Learning before teaching.
**CTA:** What’s the best advice you’ve gotten starting a new role?

### Tue Feb 18 — The Tool Sprawl Problem
Average company uses 130+ SaaS tools. Finance sees every one. Eventually.
Random charges. “Who approved this?” investigations. Duplicate tools.
Visibility isn’t a finance problem. It’s a company problem. Finance just gets stuck holding the bag.

### Thu Feb 20 — The Receipt Chase
Finance teams spend 5+ hours a week chasing receipts.
That’s not accounting. That’s babysitting.
The answer isn’t “better reminders.” It’s capturing receipts at the point of purchase — automatically.

### Fri Feb 21 — RAMP JOURNEY: Week 1 Reflection
End of week 1 at Ramp.
What I got right: Listening, asking dumb questions, not pretending.
What I got wrong: Moved too fast, compared too much, didn’t take enough notes.
The temptation is to prove you belong. The smarter move is to prove you’re coachable.

## WEEK 4: Feb 24-28 (Ramp Week 2)

### Mon Feb 24 — The “Quick Question” Tax
“Quick question—can you check if this invoice was paid?”
Each one takes 5-10 minutes. Multiply by 15 people per day. That’s the whole morning. Gone.
If people need to ask finance for information, the information isn’t accessible enough.
Self-serve data kills the quick question tax.

### Tue Feb 25 — The Approval Bottleneck
Every company has one. That person who approves everything.
They become the bottleneck for the entire company.
The fix isn’t “approve faster.” It’s smarter delegation. Tiered limits. Automated routing.
Control doesn’t require a single point of failure.

### Thu Feb 27 — The Month-End Scramble
First week of the month. Finance is in the trenches. Rest of the company has moved on.
By the time books are closed, the data is already stale.
A 5-day close isn’t a flex. It’s a necessity.

### Fri Feb 28 — RAMP JOURNEY: End of Month
Three things learned: (1) Gap between “how we’ve always done it” and “how it could be” is massive. (2) Being new is uncomfortable — that’s where growth happens. (3) Best product doesn’t matter if you can’t explain it simply.
