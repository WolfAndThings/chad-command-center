## Module 1: Why AI Interiors Fail
Before you build, understand why most AI interiors look fake.

| Problem | Looks Like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Floating furniture | Pieces hovering, no contact shadows | Add grounded/contact-shadow language |
| Plastic materials | Shiny and uniform | Specify exact material + finish + patina |
| Flat lighting | No depth or shadow play | Set time-of-day + directional light |
| Wrong proportions | Giant sofas, tiny doors | Reference real dimensions |
| Too clean | Showroom, not home | Add lived-in styling elements |
| Generic everything | Could be anywhere | Use named brands + specific location |

Your job in this course: solve all six.

## Module 3: Material Accuracy
AI fails when prompts say "wood" or "marble" generically. Use exact material language.

### Wood Library
- **White Oak:** quarter-sawn, matte, visible grain and tone variation
- **Walnut:** solid American black walnut, heartwood/sapwood contrast, oil finish
- **Reclaimed Pine:** nail holes, patina, wax finish
- **Cerused Oak:** lime-washed grain, silvered appearance
- **Teak:** golden-brown, naturally oily, weather-tolerant
- **Fumed Oak:** deep amber tone from ammonia process
- **Rift-Sawn White Oak:** tight vertical grain, modern cabinetry grade

### Stone Library
- Calacatta Borghini, Calacatta Viola, Carrara, Danby, Travertine, Soapstone, Zellige, Board-Formed Concrete, Limestone, Bluestone

### Metal Library
- Unlacquered brass, brushed nickel, matte black powder coat, oil-rubbed bronze, blackened steel, patinated brass

### Textile Library
- Belgian linen, boucle, full-grain leather, cashmere, mohair velvet, performance linen

## Module 5: Styling Systems
Two modes. Pick intentionally.

### Catalog-Clean Styling
**Use For:** New construction marketing, architecture portfolios, product-forward work.

**Include:** Minimal accessories, one hero plant, 2–3 books, one throw, intentional art.

**Exclude:** Personal clutter, visible cords/TVs, daily mess.

**Prompt Add-On:**
```txt
catalog-clean styling, minimal accessories with museum placement, surfaces mostly clear, architecture-forward presentation
```

### Lived-In Styling
**Use For:** Listings, virtual staging, hospitality, interior portfolio storytelling.

**Include:** Slightly undone bedding, open book, half-full mug, draped throw, subtle activity traces.

**Exclude:** Dirty dishes, laundry, distracting mess.

**Prompt Add-On:**
```txt
lived-in styling, subtle signs of activity, inviting and warm, someone just stepped away
```

### Decision Framework
| Client Type | Styling Mode | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Architect | Catalog-Clean | Design must lead |
| Developer | Catalog-Clean | Aspirational sales presentation |
| Real Estate Agent | Lived-In | Buyer imagination trigger |
| Interior Designer | Lived-In | Emotional feel of space |
| Furniture Brand | Catalog-Clean | Product is hero |
| Hospitality | Lived-In | Guest experience simulation |
