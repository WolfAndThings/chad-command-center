## Module 2 — Lighting & Depth
### Lessons
- Directional key light vs flat ambient
- Shadow density + contrast behavior
- Practical light integration
- Light falloff for depth

### Image Direction
![Module 2 lighting reference](images/course1_module2_lighting_reference.png)

## Module 3 — Texture, Skin, and Materials
### Lessons
- Skin micro-texture and pore realism
- Fabric/material behavior
- Imperfections and asymmetry
- Grain/noise for authenticity

### QC Checklist
- Visible skin texture
- No plastic smoothing
- Material wear is believable
- At least one imperfect detail

## Module 4 — Environment Specificity
### Lessons
- Real places and named contexts
- Prop logic and scene grounding
- Lifestyle entropy (not sterile staging)

## Module 5 — Camera Logic
### Lessons
- Focal length and perspective control
- Camera distance + framing intention
- Subject/background separation

## Module 6 — Iteration Workflow
### 3-Turn Method
1. Generate
2. Direct targeted changes
3. Refine final pass

> [!NOTE]
> Never restart from zero if composition is good—direct what exists.

## Module 7 — QC & Output Standards
### Pass/Fail Rubric
- Realism
- Composition
- Texture integrity
- Brand consistency
- Production readiness
