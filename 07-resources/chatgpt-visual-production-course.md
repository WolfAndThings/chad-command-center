## Module 1: Reference Lock & Style Control

Same photographer. Different scene.

**What You're Learning:**

### Style Extraction

**Instruction:** Find a real editorial or documentary photo, upload it to ChatGPT, then paste one prompt below.

#### OPTION A — Editorial / Brand Extraction

Analyze this image as if it were a high-end editorial shoot.

Describe:
- lighting direction, quality, and softness
- color palette and temperature
- camera distance, focal length estimate, and perspective
- contrast behavior and shadow density
- skin texture and retouching level
- environmental detail and set design
- emotional tone and energy

Return the result as a reusable "style lock" I can paste into future generation prompts.

#### OPTION B — Documentary / Real Life Extraction

Analyze this image as documentary photography taken in a real location.

Explain:
- how natural light behaves (direction, falloff, color)
- what imperfections make it believable (grain, framing, focus)
- how the framing avoids polish (crop, angle, symmetry)
- what props, clothing, or environment details ground it in reality
- why it feels human and unplanned

Turn this into a reusable style description I can paste into future generation prompts.

## Module 5: Systems & Fixes

Directing instead of hoping.

**What You're Learning:**
How to diagnose and fix weak images without starting over.

### Quick QC Fixes

**Instruction:** When an image comes out wrong, don't regenerate from scratch. Keep the same reference image uploaded and paste the relevant fix prompt below.

FEELS FAKE / TOO CLEAN:

Regenerate this image as if shot in a real Williamsburg apartment, not a studio:
- break the symmetry, nothing in real life is centered
- add natural light falloff (bright near windows, darker in corners)
- visible imperfections: scuffed floors, a water ring on the coffee table, charger cable hanging off the nightstand
- slightly off framing like the photographer was standing in a doorway
- add real brands: a Brooklinen duvet, a half-read Ottessa Moshfegh novel, an Aesop hand soap pump by the sink

FEELS STIFF / POSED:

Add movement as if someone just walked into the room:
- mid-action posture, not standing still
- hands doing something natural: adjusting a sleeve, reaching for a coffee mug, pushing hair behind an ear
- moment-in-between energy, caught between two actions
- slight motion blur on one hand or a piece of fabric
- weight shifted to one leg, not evenly distributed

FEELS GENERIC / COULD BE ANYWHERE:

Ground this in a specific real place and time:
- name the neighborhood (Silver Lake, Greenpoint, Marais, Shibuya)
- add a time of day with specific light (7am east-facing golden hour, 11pm warm lamp glow)
- add location-specific props: a Lawson convenience store bag, a Sweetgreen container, a De Cecco pasta box on the counter
- add practical light sources: a neon bar sign, a laptop screen glow, string lights on a fire escape
- if there's a window, put something real outside it (fire escape, palm tree, brick wall of the building next door)

SKIN LOOKS PLASTIC / AI:

Fix the skin to feel photographed, not rendered:
- add visible pores on the nose and cheeks
- add micro-texture: a small mole, peach fuzz catching backlight, subtle under-eye darkness
- if skin is catching light, add micro-beads of sweat or natural oil sheen
- remove any smoothing or airbrushing
- reference: "skin texture as seen through an 85mm f/1.4 lens at f/2, sharp but not clinical"

## Module 6: ChatGPT Power Features

The features most people ignore are the ones that make ChatGPT a production tool instead of a toy.

### Memory: Your Persistent Style Brain

ChatGPT remembers things between conversations. Use this.

**Setup:**
Go to Settings > Personalization > Memory. Make sure it's ON.

Tell ChatGPT your default style rules once. It remembers forever.

PASTE THIS INTO A NEW CONVERSATION:

Remember these as my default visual production rules:
- I shoot in a documentary/editorial style. Never clean, never stock.
- My images always include: specific camera + lens, directional lighting, real brand names, visible texture and grain.
- Never use these words: beautiful, stunning, perfect, 8K, hyperrealistic, masterpiece.
- Default film stock: Kodak Portra 400 for warm scenes, Fuji Pro 400H for cool scenes.
- Skin always has visible pores, texture, and imperfection.
- Every environment is grounded in a real, named location.
- When I say "lock this style" -- save the full style description for reuse.

**Why This Matters:**
Every new conversation starts with your rules baked in. No more re-explaining your preferences. You prompt shorter and get better results because ChatGPT already knows your baseline.

**Advanced Memory Moves:**

SAVE A BRAND LOCK:
"Remember this as the BEEFBOY brand lock: flash photography, smash burger food truck, red/yellow palette (#D62828/#F7B801), Kodak Portra 400 pushed, visible grease splatter, neon signage, chaotic energy."

SAVE A CHARACTER:
"Remember this character as SOFIA: mid-20s Latina woman, shoulder-length dark wavy hair, small nose ring, vintage band tees, always shot on 35mm, documentary energy."

RECALL ANYTIME:
"Generate a BEEFBOY image with SOFIA at the counter."

ChatGPT pulls both from memory. One sentence. Full production context.

### Custom GPTs: Your Personal Art Director

Custom GPTs let you build a specialized version of ChatGPT that follows your rules every single time -- no memory prompting needed.

**How To Build One:**

1. Go to ChatGPT > Explore GPTs > Create
2. Name it something useful: "LoopWorker Visual Director" or "[Your Brand] Image Engine"
3. In the Instructions field, paste your complete production rules:

**Example Instructions:**

You are a visual production art director. Every image you generate follows these rules:

STYLE: Documentary/editorial photography. Never stock, never AI-looking.
TECHNICAL: Always include specific camera body, lens, focal length, aperture, and lighting direction.
BRANDS: Always use real, named brands for props, clothing, and environment details.
TEXTURE: Visible grain, skin pores, material imperfection. Film-like rendering.
BANNED WORDS: beautiful, stunning, perfect, 8K, hyperrealistic, masterpiece, highly detailed.
LIGHTING: Always directional. Always specify source. Never flat.
ENVIRONMENT: Always grounded in a specific, named real-world location.

When the user gives a brief, you:
1. Identify the style anchor
2. Build the full prompt following the architecture: Style Anchor > Subject > Action > Context > Technical > Modifiers
3. Generate the image
4. Offer a QC fix if anything looks off

4. Save it. Pin it. Use it for all visual work.

**Why This Beats Memory:**
Memory stores facts. A Custom GPT stores behavior. It doesn't just know your rules -- it follows a workflow. Every prompt goes through the same architecture automatically.

**Build These Gpts:**
- One for your main brand style (e.g., "BEEFBOY Visual Director")
- One for client work (e.g., "Client Production Engine")
- One for UGC specifically (e.g., "UGC Generator")

Each one follows different rules. Switch between them like switching cameras.

### Multi-Turn Generation: The Iteration Workflow

The biggest mistake: generating one image and starting over if it's not right.

The move: treat every generation as a conversation. Build on what you have.

**The 3-Turn Workflow:**

TURN 1 -- GENERATE:
Paste your full prompt. Get the first image.

TURN 2 -- DIRECT:
Don't regenerate. Give specific notes on what to change:

"Keep everything but:
- Move the light source to camera left
- Add visible grain
- Make the expression less posed, more caught mid-thought
- Add a coffee cup on the table that wasn't there"

TURN 3 -- REFINE:
One more pass for polish:

"Almost perfect. Last fixes:
- Slightly warmer color temperature
- More shadow depth in the background
- The skin on the hands is too smooth -- add texture"

**Why 3 Turns Beats 1:**
- Turn 1 gets the composition and subject right
- Turn 2 dials in the mood, lighting, and details
- Turn 3 is QC -- the final 10% that makes it feel real

NEVER do this: generate, hate it, write a whole new prompt, start over. You lose everything. Instead, direct what you have. Like a real photographer reviewing a Polaroid between takes.

**Advanced Multi-Turn Moves:**

STYLE TRANSFER MID-CONVERSATION:
"Take this image and regenerate it with the lighting and color of the reference I uploaded earlier."

SERIES BUILDING:
"Now generate the same person in a different location, keeping the exact same lighting, color grade, and energy."

COMPONENT SWAP:
"Same image but change the product from the coffee mug to a whiskey glass. Keep everything else identical."

These moves are only possible because ChatGPT holds the conversation context. Each turn builds on the last. This is production, not lottery.
