## LEAD MAGNET #1: THE FIRST CALL FRAMEWORK
**"How to Win Deals in the First 5 Minutes"**

The 3-Period Framework (like a hockey game):
- Period 1: Align on Objectives (Minutes 1-2)
- Period 2: Align on Agenda (Minutes 2-4)
- Period 3: Align on Outcomes (Minutes 4-5)

Key stat: Top reps talk 46%, bottom reps talk 72% (Gong Labs, 1M+ interactions)

## LEAD MAGNET #2: THE ANNUAL SALES AUDIT
**"6 Steps to Find $50K You're Leaving on the Table"**

The 6-Step Process:
1. List Every Client (won, lost, stalled)
2. Add Two Columns (Why #1, Why #2)
3. For Each WIN — Top 2 reasons
4. For Each LOSS — Why (under 10 seconds, gut feeling)
5. Compare Patterns
6. Pick 1-3 Loss Themes -> Post-it on monitor

ROI: 45 minutes invested, potential 5-10% quota lift

## LEAD MAGNET #3: THE DISQUALIFICATION CHECKLIST
**"How to Know When to Walk Away"**

The 10 Red Flags:
1. Can't access economic buyer
2. No compelling event
3. Budget not confirmed
4. Pain isn't quantified
5. Single-threaded
6. They're price-shopping
7. Timeline is "whenever"
8. Champion has no power
9. They've been "looking" forever
10. Your gut says no

Scoring: 0-1 = proceed | 2 = dig deeper | 3-4 = consider walking | 5+ = walk away
