# ENGAGEMENT PLAYBOOK
## Daily Routine (20-25 minutes)

---

## DAILY SCHEDULE

### Morning (10-12 min)
- Respond to comments on yesterday's post (5-7 min)
- Reply to DMs from lead magnet captures (5 min)

### Lunch/Afternoon (15 min)
- Make 3-5 strategic comments on ICP posts (10 min)
- Send 2-3 connection requests to target audience (5 min)

## COMMENT TEMPLATES (Chad's Voice)

### Pattern 1: Agree + Add Data
"This. The data backs it up - [stat]. I've seen this play out in [brief example]. What's been your experience with [question]?"

### Pattern 2: Story + Lesson
"Reminded me of [brief story]. Learned that [lesson]. How do you handle [related situation]?"

### Pattern 3: Challenge + Curiosity
"Interesting take. I've always approached it as [different angle]. What made you land on [their approach]?"

**VOICE RULE:** No "I" spam. Lead with insight/data/story, not yourself.

---

## CONNECTION REQUEST TEMPLATES

### Template 1: Sales Pro
"Hey [Name], saw you're crushing it at [Company]. Love following your sales journey."

### Template 2: Mutual Interest
"Hey [Name], your post on [topic] resonated. Would love to connect."

### Template 3: Industry Peer
"Hey [Name], fellow enterprise sales. Let's connect!"

**BEST PRACTICE:** Send without note 50% of time (higher acceptance)

---

## WHO TO TARGET

**Primary:** Enterprise AEs, Sales leaders, VPs of Sales, CFOs/Controllers
**Secondary:** SDRs/BDRs, Sales coaches, B2B SaaS founders
**Avoid:** Recruiters, Random connection collectors, People with 20K+ connections
