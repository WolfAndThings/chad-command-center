## SUCCESS INDICATORS
- Comments shift from "great post" to "this helped me close a deal"
- DMs asking for advice (not just thanking for lead magnet)
- People tag you in relevant posts
- Engagement rate climbs to 3%+
- Profile views trending up week-over-week
- Discovery calls booking organically
- RAMP leadership engaging with content

---

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Low engagement?
- Try more personal/failure stories
- Ask questions at end of posts
- Engage more with ICP before posting

### No DMs?
- Respond to comments faster (within 2 hours)
- Make CTAs clearer
- Follow up on old lead magnet comments

### Wrong audience engaging?
- Adjust content mix
- Comment on CFO/finance leader content
- Use industry hashtags
